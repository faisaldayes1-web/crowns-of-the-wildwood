# Crowns of the Wildwood (work in progress)

Elves vs Humans: steal the enemy monarch and carry them to your throne. First to 2 captures wins, or the higher score when the 10 minute clock runs out.
Real low-poly models and textures (all CC0, see `assets/CREDITS.md`), with every rule and number in plain GDScript.

## Run it

1. Download Godot 4.3 or newer (Standard version, not .NET) from https://godotengine.org/download
2. Open Godot, click **Import**, and pick the `project.godot` file in this folder.
3. Press **F5** (or the play button at the top right).

**Menus on a pad.** The title screen, pause menu, settings and controls all work from a gamepad: a gold pointer appears as soon as you touch the D-pad or left stick, the D-pad (or a stick flick) jumps it to the next button, Cross / A picks, Circle / B backs out of the pause menu or the guide, and the bumpers switch tabs. The pointer hides again when the mouse moves. Promotions in the perk menu are on the bumpers.

**PS5 and other gamepads.** A DualSense (PS5) or DualShock (PS4) pad works over USB or Bluetooth with no setup, as do Xbox and other standard pads. The game reads the pad's name when it connects (a toast says what it found) and from then on every keycap, hint and the Controls tab show that pad's names and glyphs: the blue cross, red circle, pink square and green triangle for a PlayStation pad, coloured A B X Y for an Xbox one. Player 1's labels follow whatever was pressed last, so touching the keyboard brings the key names back; players 2 to 4 in couch play always see their pad's names. The DualSense rumbles when you take a hit (harder on your last heart), land a melee swing or an ability, fire a ranged shot, grab or capture the monarch, and when a castle door comes down; the **Rumble** switch in Settings turns it off, and **Button names** there forces Xbox or PlayStation names if a pad reports an odd name. Adaptive triggers and the light bar are not driven. `--debug-pad=ps` draws the HUD as if a PS5 pad were in hand, for screenshots.

**Couch play.** On SELECT MAP turn **SPLIT SCREEN** on, then in the READY UP lobby every extra gamepad presses Cross / A to join as the next player (up to four; clicking an empty place adds one too). Each player picks ELVES or HUMANS on their card (the D-pad on their pad, or a click), readies up with Cross / A (Circle / B leaves), and START MATCH waits until everyone is ready. Two players split the screen top and bottom, three or four take the quarters, and each pane has its own camera, hearts, abilities, minimap and perk menu. Player 1 keeps the keyboard and mouse (or the pad they used in the menus); players 2 to 4 keep the pad they joined with. The perk menu opens per player (R or the right stick click, or stepping on the Upgrade Station); the pause menu, chat and scoreboard are shared and belong to player 1. `--couch=N` (and `--couch-mode=coop`) starts a split-screen match from the command line for testing.

Every row above except movement by stick and aiming can be rebound: open **Controls** (Esc in a match, or O / the Options button on the title screen), click a row and press the key, mouse button or gamepad button you want. Bindings are saved to `user://controls.cfg`; **Reset to defaults** puts them back. The **Settings** tab next to it has the Sound and Music sliders, on/off switches for screen shake, damage numbers, an FPS counter, the chat log and the team rosters, the bot difficulty and a reminder of the team calls; all of it is saved too.

**Main menu.** The title shows the logo over the valley with a column of wooden buttons: **PLAY**, **CUSTOMIZE**, **SETTINGS**, **TUTORIAL** (the Guide's topics), **CREDITS** and **EXIT** (click twice); the chip top right shows your account level and opens PROGRESS. PLAY goes to **SELECT MAP**: map cards (the Wildwood; the Moonlit Wildwood at account level 10; Stonekeep Fortress, Ember Pass and Twilight Grove are locked "coming soon" cards), **DIFFICULTY** (**Casual**, **Normal**, **Hardcore**: the bots' Easy, Normal and Hard tuning), **TEAM SIZE** (1v1 to 5v5; bots fill every empty place) and **SPLIT SCREEN**. START MATCH there opens **READY UP**: up to four fighters on glowing pedestals (you and the first bots of each side when playing alone, or the joined players), each with a name plate, level, side switch and ready light; 1 or 2 also starts as Elves or Humans. **CUSTOMIZE** is **CREATE YOUR CHARACTER**: your hero in 3D beside an APPEARANCE panel (body build, skin tone, hair colour, a preview of any class on either side), HAIR, ARMOR (the Classic or Shadowborn look and a gear-rank preview), COLORS (trim) and EMBLEM (your name and banner). Hair and face styles are shown locked: the KayKit heads have one painted face and haircut for now. Everything is saved with your settings. The screens are `scripts/menu.gd` (layout and input) and `scripts/menu_stage.gd` (the 3D valley shot and the torch-lit castle hall behind the other screens); the painted frames, buttons and icons come from `tools/make_menu_art.py` (`assets/ui/menu`). `--debug-screen=title|map|character|lobby|credits|tutorial|progress`, `--debug-char-tab=N` and `--debug-lobby=N` open them for screenshots, and `--team-size=N` sets the side size for `--demo` runs.

## Rules

- **FORTIFY, then FIGHT.** Every match opens with a 20 second fortify phase (`Stats.PREP_TIME`): a wall of golden light down the river blocks both sides out, the clock reads FORTIFY, and each team digs in. Engineers build turrets, Rangers set traps, Knights and Mages raise barricades (two per team, F on your own side of the river, outside the castle walls and never in the gate lane; `Stats.BARRICADE_HITS` 8) and the bots do the same. A horn and a FIGHT! card drop the wall. Start with `-- --no-prep` to skip it.
- **Hearts.** Everyone has 4 hearts. Every hit takes one heart. Healers give hearts back.
- **Dodge.** Space: a quick dash in the direction you are moving. Nothing can hit you during it. It recharges in 2 seconds and costs a little stamina or mana. You cannot dodge while carrying the monarch.
- **Block.** Shield classes (the Knight) hold right click to raise the shield: hits from the front do nothing, but each blocked hit costs 18 stamina, holding it drains stamina, you walk at half speed and cannot attack. Run out of stamina and the shield drops.
- **Kills.** Every kill punches a KILL! banner in under the clock with the victim's class portrait and your streak (DOUBLE KILL, TRIPLE KILL, QUAD KILL, RAMPAGE), a gold burst, a chime and a rumble on the pad.
- **Hits have weight.** Every hit shoves the target (fireballs shove hardest), attackers lunge or recoil, the camera kicks when you are hit or something explodes nearby, and sparks, splinters and damage numbers show what happened.
- **Walls.** Ramps in your yard lead up to the walkway on the front wall. Walk off the front edge to drop straight down into the field.
- **Aiming.** You aim with the mouse (or the right stick) and walk with WASD, so you never have to line up by walking. The yellow pointer at your feet shows your aim; the ring shows where the cursor is.
- **Health potions.** Green bottles on mossy slabs with a breathing green rune ring and drifting sparks sit at fixed spots: the middle of the road, both flanks of the field, and one in each castle yard. Walk over one when hurt to get 2 hearts back. It returns 25 seconds later. They are dots on the minimap.
- **Blessings of Light.** Every 35 to 60 seconds a glowing pillar appears somewhere in the field (never inside a castle) and the HUD announces it. Whoever reaches it first gets a 10-second buff: **Regeneration** (a heart back every 2.5 s), **Swiftness** (30% faster) or **Might** (every attack takes an extra heart). A badge over your panel counts it down; the blessing fades after 25 seconds if nobody takes it. Bots go for them too.
- **Friend or foe.** Enemies have a dark red outline, a red shadow and a red name; teammates dark green. Whoever is under your cursor (or in your aim cone on a pad) glows bright red or bright green.
- **Classes and movesets.** You spawn as a plain Elf or Human with no gear. In your cellar five **class hats** hover over pedestals (a bark stump for Elves, a stone plinth for Humans), after Fat Princess's hat machines: a plumed helm for the Knight, a feathered cap for the Ranger, a bent wizard's hat for the Mage, a mitre for the Healer, a leather cap with goggles for the Engineer (and a dark hood for the Rogue once unlocked), each with a glow in its class colour; walk up and press F to put one on, and it transforms you. The HUD's spare ability slots stay empty until you have a class. Take another to switch; dying returns you to the plain form. Bots step onto theirs. Every class has a base ability on left click (cheap, short cooldown) and two bigger abilities on Q and E that cost a lot more stamina or mana (since 2026-10-06 about 15% cheaper and, after the 2026-10-07 pass, 40% quicker to recharge, with mana regenerating 9 a second and stamina 16, and the stamina base attacks 20% cheaper so a Knight or Ranger never runs dry on plain swings; every kill also gives 20 energy back and takes 2 seconds off both cooldowns, so a good fight chains into the next ability). The two factions play the same five roles with different kits (`Stats.FACTION_KITS`, merged over `Stats.ROLES` by `Stats.kit`):
  - **Knight** (stamina). Human: **Sword Strike**, the best at breaking doors; **Block** on right click; heavy plate turns about two hits in five (0.40). **Q Shield Bash** (4 s, 35): dash forward, hitting and shoving everyone in the way. **E Shield Wall** (7 s, 40): nothing gets through for 1.8 seconds, from any side. Elf: **Glaive**, a longer, quicker sweep (2.5 m, 0.5 s) with lighter armour (0.32); **Q Wind Dash** (3.5 s, 30), a 6 m dash that cuts for a heart; **E Barkskin** (7 s, 35), 1.6 seconds of turning every blow.
  - **Ranger** (stamina). Human: **Crossbow**, heavy bolts (14 m, 0.75 s, 12 stamina). **Q Heavy Bolt** (4 s, 40): one wound-up bolt for two hearts. **E Caltrops** (6 s, 35): two spreads that hurt and slow for 2.5 s. Elf: **Moonbow**, swift silver arrows (16 m, 0.55 s, 10 stamina); **Q Starfall** (5 s, 40): six arrows in a fan; **E Vine Snare** (6 s, 35): a living snare that roots the first enemy on it for 2.5 s. Traps stop at the first wall or crate between you and the spot, so they never land inside one.
  - **Mage** (mana). Human: **Arcane Bolt**, a fast bolt with a small splash. **Q Fireball** (5 s, 55): a big ball of fire, 2 hearts in a wide splash, four hits to a door. **E Blink** (3 s, 30): teleport 6 units in the aim direction. Elf: **Thorn Bolt**, a seed that bursts into thorns and slows (0.8 s); **Q Bramble Burst** (5 s, 50): one heart to everyone in a wide splash and roots them for 1.3 s; **E Fae Step** (4 s, 30): a 7.5 m blink.
  - **Healer** (mana). Human: **Prayer** heals every hurt teammate around you (5 m), yourself included; with nobody to heal it fires a holy bolt. **Q Blessing** (8 s, 60): heal everyone nearby 2 hearts, speed them up 3 s and shield them for a second. **E Holy Bubble** (8 s, 45): a dome of light over you and every teammate within 3 m; nothing can hurt anyone inside it for 2 seconds. Elf: **Grove Mend** reaches further (6 m, 0.7 s); **Q Spirit Bloom** (8 s, 60): 2 hearts to everyone nearby and 4 s of haste; **E Lunar Lance** (3 s, 30): a bolt of moonlight that slows for 1.5 s.
  - **Engineer** (stamina). Human: **Hammer**, a heavy swing that takes three hits off a door. **Q Build Turret** (6 s, 45): a bolt turret 1.8 m in front of you, on your castle walls or grounds (up to 14 m outside the front wall, never in the door lane, never in the cellar); two at a time, the oldest makes way. **E Tune Up** (4 s, 35): repair the nearest of your turrets and raise it a level, or stand by your broken door to knock 12 seconds off its rebuild. Elf: **Root Maul**; **Q Thorn Totem**: a living totem whose thorns slow for a second (slower shots); **E Tend**: grow it a level or hurry the door's regrowth.
  - **The power curve.** Your base attack is meant to win most fights: base attacks cost 6 to 13 energy, the Q and E abilities 25 to 65, so with 16 stamina or 9 mana a second a special is something you save up for. Ranks are mild on purpose (each cuts 8% off a cooldown and cost, rank 2 widens the effect 20%, rank 3 adds a heart of healing or door damage but no extra damage; Vigor is +5% speed and +12 energy a rank), so a plain soldier who lands their hits can hold their own against a max-rank veteran, and dying (which resets you to a base soldier) is a setback rather than a knockout.
- **Stamina and mana.** Every attack costs some, and both refill slowly (11 stamina or 7 mana a second), so the Q and E abilities are a budget, not a rotation. Humans refill faster; Elves move faster.
- **Account progression.** A second experience bar belongs to you, not your life: every match banks its experience (plus 300 for a win, 150 for a draw, 100 for a loss) into your account, saved between sessions. Account levels start at 400 XP and cost 150 more each (`Stats.ACCOUNT_XP_*`, 50 levels) and carry rank titles from Recruit through Soldier, Champion, Warlord and Legend to Immortal. The title's level chip, the PROGRESS page and the end screen show your level, bar and the last match's gain. **Account level 10 unlocks** the **Rogue** class (daggers, Shadow Dash, Smoke Bomb; promotions Assassin and Nightrunner; its hood sits locked in the cellar until then), the **Shadowborn** hero look (dusk-tinted armour, violet rim light and a cape on every class) and the **Moonlit Wildwood** night map (moonlight, mist, glow). Nothing you unlock is stronger; it is more.
- **Banners.** Your banner is a calling card: a patterned background, an emblem, a frame, a title and your account level. Edit it on the EMBLEM tab of Create Your Character (some titles, the Royal frame and the Rogue emblem need levels). When an enemy kills you, their banner drops in under KILLED BY with the weapon that did it; bots carry banners dealt from their name and class.
- **Experience, per life.** Hitting (10 a heart), killing (40, with a KILL popup), healing a teammate (8 a heart), hitting the door, grabbing the monarch (25) and capturing (100) earn experience. Levels come at 40, 100, 180, 280 and 400 and each gives one rank point. Press R for the perk menu: three ranks each in your base attack, Q, E and Vigor. Rank 1 cuts the cooldown 15% and the cost 12%, rank 2 widens the effect (range, radius, duration, two more arrows), rank 3 adds a heart of damage or healing; each Vigor rank is +7% speed, +15 max energy, +20% regen. Dying wipes it all, so staying alive is how you get strong. Bots rank up too.
- **Rank gear.** Every rank point you spend in a class (its total upgrades, kept across lives) steps its look up a tier, rank 1 to 4 like the class sheet: Knights go from badge shield to round, tower and spiked shields with a growing helmet plume, pauldrons, a cape and gold trim; Rangers carry a built bow with a nocked arrow (the packs only have crossbows) that gains team wraps, gold-and-leaf tips and a glowing grip, plus a hood feather, spare arrows and a cape; Knight swords gain a rune line, a gold guard and pommel, then a gem; Mage staves gain crystals, a gold ring and a crowning crystal; Healer wands gain a crystal and a gold ring before the rank-4 cross staff; Mages a hat gem and then a ring of crystals; Healers a halo, wings and finally a staff. `tools/showcase.gd -- --ranks=0|1` renders the grid.
- **Promotions (class variants).** Every rank point you spend counts towards your *total upgrades* for the match, per class, and those are kept across lives. Spend 3 in a class and its two variants unlock in the perk menu (press 5 or 6, or click): the pick is kept for the rest of the match and you can switch any time. Like Fat Princess's upgraded hat machines, a promotion changes the base attack, Q and E, and the look:
  - **Knight → Vanguard** (greatsword, no shield): **Q Cleave** spins and hits everyone around you, **E Charge** is a long knockdown dash. **Knight → Warden** (tower shield, mace): **Q Shield Slam** pins everyone it hits for 1.5 s, **E Bulwark** is a 3 s shield wall that also shields teammates within 4 m.
  - **Ranger → Sharpshooter** (longbow, 20 m range): **Q Piercing Shot** flies through everyone in line, **E Snipe** takes two hearts. **Ranger → Trapper**: poison arrows slow, **Q Trap Line** plants three traps in a row, **E Smoke Bomb** hides you from bots and speeds you up for 3 s.
  - **Mage → Pyromancer**: burning bolts that splash, **Q Inferno** is a huge fireball, **E Flame Wave** burns everyone in a cone in front. **Mage → Frostweaver**: frost bolts slow, **Q Ice Burst** freezes everyone near the blast for 1.3 s, **E Blink** goes 8 m.
  - **Engineer → Artificer**: three **Rapid Turrets** at a time, **E Overclock** doubles their fire rate for 6 s. **Engineer → Siegewright**: a **Sledge** worth five door hits, a harness that turns about every fourth hit, **Q Ballista** turrets whose bolts burst and reach further, **E Fortify** tunes a turret or mends your door by 60 hits.
  - **Healer → Cleric**: wider Mend, **Q Sanctuary** heals, speeds and shields the group, **E Radiance** is a bursting bolt. **Healer → Dark Priest**: drain bolts heal you a heart per hit, **Q Curse** hurts and slows every enemy around you, **E Smite** takes two hearts.
- **Turrets.** An Engineer's turret is a crossbow on a timber post with a stone base and a team pennant. It shoots the nearest enemy in range (the enemy carrier first); hits and kills earn the builder XP. Level 1 soaks 8 hits, reaches 8 m and fires every 1.5 s; Tune Up takes it to level 2 (12 hits, 9.5 m, 1.2 s, iron bands) and level 3 (16 hits, 11 m, 0.95 s, gold cap and a glow). (The first numbers tried, 10/16/22 hits and 9 to 13 m, made every bot match a 0-0 draw: turrets out-killed every class.) Enemies bump into it and can shoot it, hit it or splash it (a Fireball does 2); its own team walks through. Wrecking one is worth 30 XP. Each team can have 6 standing. Turrets show as diamonds on the minimap. `Stats.TURRET` has the numbers.
- **Overtime.** A tie at full time is not a draw yet: both doors come down and stay down, nobody respawns, the clock gets 120 seconds, and the next capture or the last team with anyone standing wins. Still tied with both sides alive after that, it is a draw. Bots stop guarding their door in overtime and go for the other castle. (With respawns on, six-seed bot batches ended half their matches as overtime draws; sudden death makes the brawl decide it.)
- **Kill feed.** The last few kills show under the logo, killer and victim with class icons in team colours.
- **Teams and scoreboard.** The live HUD keeps the screen clear: the minimap sits top-left, the score and clock up top, the logo top-right and your own panel at the bottom. The side rosters (portrait, class, hearts, energy, class emblem) are off by default; N shows them, and the Settings tab remembers the choice. Hold Tab for the full scoreboard: both teams with everyone's class, hearts (or respawn countdown), blessing, level, score, kills, deaths, captures, hearts healed, damage and total upgrades, score = kills ×10 + assists ×4 + captures ×100 + heals ×5 + damage ×2 + upgrades ×3. Hitting someone a teammate then kills within 8 seconds is an assist (15 XP). Enter opens team chat (`/all` for both teams) in a panel styled after the reference render: All and Team tabs, a row per message with the speaker's portrait, their name in team colour and the match clock, and an input box with a send arrow; recent rows fade over the field while you play. Bots answer and call out captures, broken doors and kills, and the log doubles as an event feed. Bots have names.
- **Balance notes (latest pass).** Twelve full bot matches with seeded runs (`-- --demo --seed=N`, a `RESULT` and `STAT` line per unit at the end) showed the Humans winning three of six and the Elves none: their 1.3× stamina and mana regeneration was worth far more than the Elves' 10% speed, because regeneration is what limits attack rate. Humans regenerated 1.12× against the Elves' 8% speed and the next six matches came out 85 kills to 84; once raids rallied, escorted and planted banners the Humans were back to winning two of three (353 kills to 290, 604 hearts healed to 387), so regeneration is 1.06× now. The siege itself was fixed in three steps found by reading the match logs: bots targeted enemies through walls and stood still (line of sight), broken doors rebuilt behind raids (the siege rule), and raiders arrived one at a time (the rally), and the first six-seed batch after that still had three of six end as overtime draws; demo tallies of turret kills (0 to 4 a match) and deaths inside the enemy castle showed the turrets were not the killer, the castle respawn was, hence the war banners and sudden-death overtime. With both, six seeds gave six decisive matches: three regulation captures and three overtime wipe-outs inside 15 to 48 seconds. Knights were dying twice as often as anyone: they run 6% faster and Shield Bash, Shield Slam and Charge take two hearts. The Dark Priest's drain bolt costs 27 mana and Curse cools down in 12 s. Doors take 200 hits, carriers move at 80% speed. Earlier notes: base attacks are cheap and quick (Knight sword 0.55 s, Ranger shot 12 stamina, Mage bolt 0.65 s with a small splash), the big Q abilities cost more and come back slower (Fireball 7 s / 55 mana, Volley 6 s, Shield Bash 5 s), the escapes are cheaper (Blink 25 mana, Dodge 12), every rank cuts cooldowns 12%, kills are worth 40 XP, and the promotion abilities were tuned the same way (Cleave 6 s, Snipe 10 s, Inferno 8 s / 60 mana, Ice Burst 6 s with a 1 s freeze, Curse 10 s, Dark Smite 7 s / 45 mana). Everything lives in `scripts/stats.gd`. The faction-kit pass (Elves and Humans with different weapons and spells, the Holy Bubble, cheaper base attacks and dearer specials, rank bonuses cut from 15%/12%/+1 heart to 8%/8%/no damage heart) ran its own six-seed batch; see the commit history for the tallies. After the faction kits and the FORTIFY phase, two six-seed batches went Humans 5, draw 1 and Humans 5, Elves 1 (elf doors fell first in most matches, the Human Warden out-fought the Elf Warden 46 kills to 25, the Elf Siegewright died 46 times in three matches), so the Knights' plate is closer now (Humans 0.36, Elves 0.32) and Human regeneration is 1.03×: the batch after that went Elves 4, Humans 2, and the six seeds run after the world overhaul (no script errors, no stuck bots round the new blockers) went 3 and 3 with 350 kills to 328 and four of six decided in regulation. The Siegewright still dies most (78 deaths for 29 kills across four Human Siegewrights), so its siege harness now turns about every fourth hit (`"armour": 0.25`); the six seeds after that went 3 and 3 again with Human Siegewrights down to 12 deaths a match from 20. The 2026-10-07 pass (cheap stamina attacks, 200-hit doors) tilted six seeds to the Elves 6-0 (227 kills to 173): with stamina no longer the limit on attack rate, the Humans' regeneration edge stopped mattering and the Elves' speed and reach decided every raid, so Human regeneration is 1.12x again, Human plate 0.40 and the Elves run at 6.3 instead of 6.5. The six seeds after the retune went Humans 4, Elves 2, every match decided by captures in regulation between three and eight and a half minutes.
- **Inside the castles.** Each keep is furnished: warm room lights that cast real shadows (no hanging chandeliers: the keeps are open to the sky, so the light comes from wall torches, candle stands, braziers and crystals), side rugs, a laid feasting table a side with benches, tapestries with the faction crest, bookcases in the back corners, braziers and candle stands (mushrooms, crystals and fireflies on the elven side). The spawn cellars have beams carrying two chandeliers, shelves by the spawn ring, bookcases and a rug.
- **Castles, in layers.** From the back: the **spawn cellar** (a protected sanctuary under the keep: you respawn there, the six class hats 3.2 m apart on two rows of a 14 by 19 m hall (grown 2026-10-07), the Upgrade Station pad that opens your perks, and the Wildwood Guide live there; enemies, their shots and their spells can never enter, and a flight of stairs plus a sealed back arch are its exits), the **keep** with its rooms around the **throne room** (the Crown Vault), the open **yard** with stairs up to the walls, and the **door** at the front. Each castle is an outer castle wall ringing a yard, with the keep standing inside at the back. The wall's one wooden door only blocks the enemy team, breaks after enough hits (Knights and Mages break it fastest; a Fireball is worth four hits), and rebuilds itself 40 seconds later once no enemy is left inside (the countdown holds while any enemy is inside the castle or within 9 m of the doorway, so a siege keeps the breach open and the label reads UNDER SIEGE; before that rule, bot raids broke the door and then trickled in one at a time while it rebuilt behind them) (320 door hits, `Stats.GATE_HITS`: even a full squad needs about 35 seconds of focus, so breaking in is a team job and defenders get time to answer). Ramps in the yard at both ends of the front wall lead up to the ramparts: from up there Rangers and Mages shoot down at anyone outside, and arrows from the ground cannot reach them. Mage splash still can. The keep has an open archway facing the door and is laid out as rooms around the throne room: an entrance hall with braziers and tapestries, the great hall down one side (feasting table, hearth with a fire, casks), the chapel (altar under a stained window) or the Elves' moon shrine (a glowing pool ringed with stones) down the other, and the royal study and armoury behind; every room has its own floor (flags, planks, carpet, moss), panelled walls and patterned rugs. Bots path round the throne room through the galleries.
- **Cover.** Barricades, boulders and trees in the middle stop arrows and spells. Duck behind them. Rangers (and Ranger bots) tuck in behind them.
- **Knight plate.** Knights and their promotions shrug off about every third hit (`armour` 0.34 in `Stats.ROLES`): the hit reads ARMOUR with a clang and costs no heart. It stacks with the home-defence resist. Added because Vanguards were dying twice as often as anyone else once turrets covered the yard.
- **Team calls.** Z, X and C call your team: **Attack!** sends every bot at the enemy door now (no waiting at the rally), **Defend!** brings three home to hold the castle, **To me!** brings the two nearest to where you called. The call goes out as a team chat line with a ring at your feet and a horn, a bot answers, and the squad planner follows it for 15 seconds (`Stats.COMMAND_TIME`).
- **Objective compass.** A gold arrow on the ground at your feet points at what to do next: the enemy door while it stands, the vault lock, their monarch, your throne while you carry, or the thief when your own monarch is taken. It hides within 6 m.
- **Feedback.** Your last heart puts a pulsing red vignette round the screen (a faint one on two hearts), every hit flashes the frame and draws a red arc on the side it came from, ability slots burst a gold ring the moment a cooldown ends, a CAPTURE! card (or THEY SCORED) drops under the clock when either team scores, and the match-end screen names the MVP (highest score on either team) above the table.
- **Spawn protection.** For 3 seconds after you spawn (and the whole time you stand in your own cellar) nothing can hurt you: a SPAWN PROTECTED plate shows, and hits read PROTECTED. It ends when the timer runs out or you leave the cellar.
- **Defender zone.** Inside your own castle you get DEFENDING HOME: +10% damage resistance (every tenth hit is shrugged off as FORTIFIED), +10% healing received, +10% regeneration and 15% faster door repairs. Attackers never get it, and it stops at the castle wall. Values are in `Stats.DEFENDER`.
- **Throne room (the Crown Vault).** The enemy monarch sits on a canopied throne on a two-step dais in a walled throne room at the heart of the keep: stone walls, a glowing window, tapestries, candle stands and a treasury chest in each back corners for the Humans; bark walls, moss, crystals and fireflies for the Elves. Its double doors (iron-banded oak, or living bark bound with crystal) only block the enemy; attackers have to break the lock (14 hits, `Stats.VAULT_HITS`; a Fireball is four) before they can grab the monarch, which takes a Knight about 7 seconds (it was 20; with turrets covering the yard the longer lock meant nobody got the crown out in regulation). The lock shows its hits left, sparks on every hit, swings open with a flash, and locks again 30 seconds after the keep is clear and the monarch is home.
- **CROWN STOLEN.** When the monarch is grabbed, a red card under the clock names the thief with a minimap inset, the carrier gets a golden glow and a trail, and the carrier always shows on the minimap.
- **Veterans and bounties.** 5 kills without dying makes you a VETERAN (announced, a gold ring). 10 makes you an ELITE VETERAN: a bounty is announced to the enemy, you are revealed on their minimap with a pulsing ring and a beacon over your head, and you get an extra heart and 25% faster regeneration. Whoever kills an elite earns 80 XP and the whole team gets a 10-second Blessing of Might. Dying ends the streak.
- **Death.** Respawn takes 7 seconds plus 1 per level you had (up to 13), and you come back as a plain Elf or Human at level 1 with no perks: you relevel, promotions excepted.
- **Wildwood Guide.** A friendly NPC in each cellar near the stations. Walk up (a speech bubble greets you), press F to talk: a dialogue box with a big portrait walks you through the game, then a topic menu (objective, fighting, classes, upgrades, the Crown, where to go; press 1-6 or click). Esc closes.
- **Bots.** Bots play the objective: a squad planner hands out jobs every second (recover our stolen crown, escort our carrier, defend the castle when the gate is hurt or the keep breached, otherwise attack), Knights frontline and cover healers, Rangers keep range and use cover, Mages save Fireballs for groups, Healers stick with the carrier or the most hurt and back off from melee, and anyone outnumbered and hurt falls back to a teammate. Each team's lineup is Knight, Ranger, Mage, Healer and Engineer: the bot Engineer builds on the rampart either side of the gatehouse, then in the yard, tunes everything to level 3, then takes the hammer to the enemy door (the planner still calls it home when the keep is breached). A hurt gate now pulls one bot home, a breached keep two, so a team is never four defenders and one attacker. Raiders rally: anyone on the attack gathers on the road 20 m outside the enemy door (past the reach of rampart turrets) and waits up to 14 seconds for a second raider before pushing; a broken door or overtime means push now (`Stats.RALLY`). Before the rally, raids arrived one bot at a time and fed the turrets.
- **Monarch.** Walk up to the enemy monarch and press F to pick them up. You move slower and cannot attack while carrying. Reach the gold ring around your own throne to score. If the carrier dies, the monarch walks back home; touching your own dropped monarch sends them home instantly.

## The look

Real models and textures, all CC0 (attributions in `assets/CREDITS.md`):

- **Characters** are the KayKit Adventurers models (Knight, Rogue, Hooded Rogue, Mage, Barbarian) with their own animations: idle, run, sword swings, crossbow shots, spellcasting, blocking, dodging, hit reactions and deaths. `scripts/character_model.gd` picks the model and gear per class, applies a team-colour skin (made by `tools/recolor_skins.py` from the pack's palette textures: green and blond for Elves, blue for Humans, white robes for Healers), adds elf ears and the monarchs' crowns, and runs the animation state machine.
- **The world beyond the walls** (2026-10-06 overhaul): a village on each castle's north flank in the team's colour (cottages, a barrel-tavern, a market, a well, a church and a blacksmith for the Kingdom, a lumber mill and an archery range for the Forest) with chimney smoke, fences and lantern posts; a farm on the south flank with fields of wheat, a windmill and pumpkins; a mine or lumber camp behind each cellar with mountains on the horizon; lantern posts and benches along the road; lilies and reeds in the river with sandy banks; moss beds under the Wildwood trees; autumn pines in the Kingdom's woods; and the old barrow in the Wildwood (a crypt, leaning stones, a dead tree and candles that never go out). A watermill with a turning paddle wheel in the river stands on each team's bank, campfires burn in the work camps (the Kingdom drills at targets beside a half-built wall; the Forest clears stumps and stacks planks), low stones dot the field and slow clouds drift over the mountains and the far hills. The Forest's village, farm and camp are built in the trees: elven tree-houses (`_add_treehouse`: a living trunk with spiral steps, a round plank platform with a rope rail, a bark hut with a lit window under a cone of leaves, a glowing crown and a hanging lantern) replace the cottages, with elven lanterns, mushrooms and fireflies in the lane. Both farms keep a flock of sheep in a paddock and chickens scratch about the village lanes; butterflies drift over the flower patches, birds circle high over the field and leaves fall through the Wildwood (pale mint and lavender) and the Kingdom's autumn woods (orange); all of it is `scripts/critter.gd`, primitives that wander inside a pen or orbit a point and never touch the fight. Keeps are furnished with KayKit tables, chairs, shelves, kegs and a treasury of coin; cellars with bunks and stocked shelves. Keep floors are marble (Humans) and rugs use a woven carpet texture.
- **Solid clutter, no fences inside.** Every crate, barrel, rack, keg, table, shelf and chest inside the castles (yard, keep and cellar) gets a box collider the size of its model when it is placed (`_solidify_prop` in `scripts/game.gd`), so nobody walks through the furniture; rugs, coins and anything hung on a wall stay walkable. There is nothing fence-like inside the walls: no railing along the rampart's inner edge or the yard stairs, barricades go up outside the walls only, and the villages and farms have no fence rows along their lanes (the sheep keep their paddock). The bots' yard turret spot and the cellar bunks are placed so nobody gets wedged (a six-seed batch is checked for bots holding position with a snapshot pass).
- **Ground textures**: the grass is a clean two-green meadow with a fine nap and no painted marks (flowers, tufts and stones are placed by the game, and sparingly), and the road and verges are packed earth with flat worn stones, with nothing directional in them so the triplanar mapping tiles cleanly (the old road texture carried ruts and grass edges that repeated three times across the road).
- **Graphics and resolution** (2026-10-07): the game opens in a 1920x1080 window that can be resized or made fullscreen (Settings), and the HUD scales sharply with it (canvas-items stretch from a 1280x720 layout, so a 4K screen gets 4K text and a 4K world). Settings has a GRAPHICS preset: LOW (FXAA, 80% render scale with FSR upscaling, small soft shadows, no ambient occlusion or bloom) for older laptops, MEDIUM (2x MSAA, ambient occlusion, bloom), HIGH, the default (4x MSAA, 8K sun shadows with soft PCSS penumbras, screen-space ambient occlusion and indirect bounce light, soft-light bloom, a gentle tilt-shift blur at the far edge of the view, 16x anisotropic texture filtering) and ULTRA (8x MSAA, volumetric light in the air).
- **World**: hand-styled cream ashlar (plain and moss-and-ivy), sandstone flagstone (plain, cracked and mossy), cobble, rock, shingle, nailed plank, bark, grass and sandy road textures with normal maps, generated by `tools/make_textures.py` and applied triplanar so every wall and floor tiles cleanly. Both castles are built from one kit (`_add_wall`, `_add_tower`, `_add_stairs`, `_add_railing`, `_add_banner`, `_add_rug` in `scripts/game.gd`): cream ashlar walls with merlons, square corner towers and a gatehouse with pyramid roofs in the team colour, a timber rampart with a railing, timber stairs along the side walls, a banded double door, flagstone yards, team rugs and banners, shingled roofs, and no fences inside the walls. The two castles are built from the same kit in different materials: the Human one is cream ashlar with merlons, pyramid roofs and bracket torches; the elven one is *grown*, with living-bark walls, a vine ledge and leaf tufts for merlons, tree-trunk towers crowned with glowing canopies and hung with teal lanterns, glowing crystals in the wall brackets, living trunk pillars in the keep and glowing mushrooms in the yard instead of stores. The Wildwood half of the field has larger pale-mint and lavender canopies that glow faintly, mushroom rings, fireflies and more pines. Every prop is placed with clearance from the walls, and `--audit` (`godot --headless -- --audit`) lists any prop that still overlaps a wall, fence, gate or another prop; a bright river with a shader-animated surface (painted ripple crescents, foam along the banks and the island, lily pads) cuts the field in two between stone-kerbed banks, crossed by two bridges built from the same kit (ashlar abutments, timber stringers, individual planks and railings) and the shrine island; cobbled roads with dirt verges run gate to gate. Elven trees are procedural: twisted trunks in deep-fissured bark, roots, layered canopies with a wind-swaying leaf shader (painted tufts, a sunlit crown and a dark rim), stacked-cone pines, glowing blossoms and autumn variants; the human side mixes in KayKit oaks and pines. Field cover cycles three designs (supply stacks, sharpened timber palisades, broken ashlar wall stubs with rubble); the ruins are small halls with broken merloned walls, standing and fallen columns, rubble and ivy; the roads carry two ashlar milestones a side and timber signposts at the path bends (the roadside caravan pile was clutter and went). Mossy faceted boulders, bushes, fireflies, and thousands of MultiMesh flowers, grass tufts, stones, mushrooms and fallen leaves (kept off the lanes) fill the ground. The Crown Shrine sits on a stone island in the river: a two-tier ashlar dais with a gold inlay ring, kit rim walls, and stone stairs with railings from both banks. Characters get a dark outline and rim light so they read against the ground. KayKit Medieval Hexagon and Dungeon props still provide crates, barrels, racks, targets, banners, torches and columns, repainted to the same warm palette (`tools/recolor_palette.py`, and the dungeon stone textures tinted to the castle's sandstone).
- **Sound**: every clip is synthesized from scratch by `tools/make_sounds.py` (numpy: tuned sines, filtered noise and envelopes, nothing sampled, so nothing to license): 57 effects (swings and hits, bow twangs and arrow thwacks, bolts, fireballs, frost, heals and blessings, hurt and death, dodge, block, footsteps on grass and stone, door hits, the door crash and rebuild, the vault lock, crown grab, drop and capture fanfares, level, rank and promotion chimes, turret clanks, horns, menu clicks), a 12-second wind, brook and birdsong ambience loop, and a 40-second original theme in D dorian (melody with vibrato over a bass drone, harp arpeggios and a soft drum) that plays on the title screen and quietly under the match. `scripts/sfx.gd` pools 3D players (sounds fade with distance from the listener on your character; repeats within 40 ms merge) and UI players, and the Controls tab has Sound and Music sliders (saved with the settings).
- **Effects**: every attack and ability has its own projectile model and trail (arrows, spinning arcane orbs, fireballs, holy bolts), burst rings, light flashes, particle splashes, knockback, recoil, camera shake, damage numbers and level-up pillars; all built in `scripts/game.gd` (`spawn_splash`, `spawn_ring`, `spawn_pillar`, `spawn_flash`) and `scripts/projectile.gd`.
- `assets/ui/logo.png` is the game's logo, `assets/ui/cards/` holds the Elves and Humans faction logos, crests and the ten class portraits (the Engineer has no portrait yet and uses the drawn face) (used on the title screen, the score tabs, the Tab panel, the player panel and the game menu), and `assets/ui/icons/` holds the painted icon set (every attack and ability, dodge, block, grab, potion, blessings, class emblems and the Forest and Kingdom crests) drawn by `tools/make_icons.py`. `scripts/hud.gd` draws everything else after the UI references: score tabs with the faction crests, the Tab teams panel, class emblems and levels, the minimap, the player panel (portrait with level badge, hearts, energy, a glowing experience bar that pulses when a rank point is waiting, slots for the base attack, Q, E, dodge, block and grab), the rank menu, the game menu (overview map, class cards, controls), the title screen and the victory ribbon.
- `tools/showcase.gd` renders every character to a PNG for a quick art check:

```
godot --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1600x700 --script tools/showcase.gd -- --out=showcase.png
```

## Tuning the game

All the numbers are in one file: `scripts/stats.gd`. Hearts per player, damage per hit, stamina and mana costs, every ability's cooldown and cost, ranges, door strength, health potion value and respawn, blessing timings, respawn time, match length, captures to win. Change a number, press F5, and it is live.

## Code layout

- `scripts/stats.gd` every balance number, the class table, the ten promotions, the turret table and the score weights.
- `scripts/game.gd` builds the map, spawns teams, runs scoring, the timer and the match flow.
- `scripts/unit.gd` is every soldier: classes, combat, player controls and the bot brain.
- `scripts/gate.gd` the breakable castle gate; `scripts/vault.gd` the Crown Vault lock; `scripts/barricade.gd` a breakable timber fence (kept for field cover, none inside the bases); `scripts/guide.gd` the Wildwood Guide NPC.
- `scripts/projectile.gd` arrows, spells and bolts.
- `scripts/heal_orb.gd` the health potions on the map.
- `scripts/blessing.gd` the Blessings of Light that spawn in the field.
- `scripts/trap.gd` the Ranger's snare trap; `scripts/turret.gd` the Engineer's turrets; `scripts/seal.gd` the class hats in the cellars.
- `scripts/sfx.gd` all sound: pooled positional and UI players, ambience and music.
- `scripts/hud.gd` the on-screen HUD, minimap, chat, scoreboard, perk and pause menus (with control remapping), title and end screens.
- `scripts/character_model.gd` the animated characters and monarchs built from the KayKit models.
- `tools/showcase.gd` renders all characters to a PNG; `tools/recolor_skins.py` makes the team skins; `tools/make_textures.py`, `tools/make_icons.py`, `tools/make_sounds.py` and `tools/recolor_palette.py` generate the textures, HUD icons, sounds and prop palette.
- `assets/` models, textures, skins, logo and `CREDITS.md`.
- `scripts/monarch.gd` the Elf Queen and Human King.

To watch bots play each other (handy for balance testing), run from a terminal:
`godot --path . -- --demo` (add `--play` instead to jump straight into a match as the Elves).
For balance batches run headless with a seed, `godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 --quit-after 45000 -- --demo --seed=7`: the log carries a `t=` snapshot every 30 s (score, doors, turrets, every unit's position and job), `Door broken`, `Capture` and `Overtime` events, then `RESULT winner=... score=... t=... overtime=... turrets=...` and a `STAT` line per unit (class, kills, deaths, assists, damage, healing, captures, level). `--debug-time=N` starts the clock at N seconds (overtime testing), `--debug-turrets` gives the player an Engineer with a turret of each level.
