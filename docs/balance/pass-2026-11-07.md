## What was run
- 150 seeded all-bot 4v4 matches (`--demo`), headless, `tools/balance/runbatch.sh` (63000-frame cap), DIFF=Normal, every map, seeds 101-110 per map, in three batches of 50:
  - `base`: main as it is.
  - `tuneA`: Vanguard armour 0.50 **and** Human Crossbow cooldown 0.62 to 0.58 (tried, then dropped: no effect on the Human ranged numbers).
  - `tuneB`: Vanguard armour 0.50 only (what this branch ships).
- New tally `tools/balance/winrates.py` (beside `agg.py`, unchanged): per-map faction win rate and match length, and per class the win rate of the side that fielded it (draw = half). A bot's class is the variant it ended on (bots promote at random once they have 3 rank points).
- Evidence: `docs/evidence/crowns-balance-1107/` (RESULT and STAT lines of all 150 logs, which `winrates.py` and `agg.py` read directly with `OUT=docs/evidence/crowns-balance-1107/logs`; full tallies `*_winrates.md`, `*_agg.txt`, `pooled150_winrates.md`; test logs).

**Read the numbers this way:** 4v4 bots always field Knight, Ranger, Mage and Healer on both sides (`game.gd` `LINEUP`), so a class *line* is 50% by construction. What carries meaning is variant against variant (Vanguard v Warden, Cleric v Dark Priest, ...). Engineer only appears from 5 a side and Rogue never appears in bot lineups, so neither can be judged from bot matches.

## Findings
1. **Vanguard was the class outlier and the change helps.** Before: 40% against the Warden's 60%, the worst K/D in the game (0.46) and the most deaths (13.4 a match: it has no shield, so no block). With armour 0.50 (turns every other hit, Warden/Knight plate is 0.37) its K/D rose in both batches that carried it (0.56 and 0.57), deaths fell to about 12, and in `tuneB` it won 53% to the Warden's 47%. The win rates are inside the noise; the K/D and deaths move the same way twice.
2. **Faction totals swing far more between batches than they should, so a single 50-match batch cannot judge faction balance.** Elves won 63%, 61% and 33% in three batches whose changes were symmetric or nearly so; pooled it is 52% (76-69, 5 draws). The runs are not fully repeatable: economy calm timers and the world-build slicing read the real clock (`Time.get_ticks_msec`, `economy.gd`, `game.gd`), and 15 matches ran at once. No faction change is proposed; claude-faisal3's 11-04 finding (factions even) still holds on the pooled data.
3. **Stonekeep favours the Humans in every batch** (35, 25, 35% Elves; 32% pooled over 30). That is a map/side pattern, not a class one. Twilight Grove leans Elves (68% pooled) but it swung from 80 to 50.
4. **Match length is on target.** 147 of 150 matches finished between 12 and 17 minutes (900 s clock plus up to 120 s overtime); the average is 15 minutes because almost every match runs to the clock (bots rarely reach 8 captures). Three matches ended early on 8-0 routs: Ember Pass 695 s (`tuneA`), Ember Pass 642 s and Stonekeep 480 s (`tuneB`). None before the change; three in 100 is too few to blame on it, but watch Ember Pass, the map with the most captures.
5. **Dark Priest is the next candidate.** 51% before, 31% after, 41% pooled against the Cleric's 57%, despite a K/D as good as the Cleric's. It heals less (its Drain Bolt costs 21 mana against Mend's 16) and has no Sanctuary shield.

## Proposed (Faisal decides)
- **A. Applied on this branch:** Vanguard armour 0.45 -> 0.50.
- **B. Not applied, needs its own batch:** Dark Priest Drain Bolt cost 21 -> 18 (`VARIANTS[Role.HEALER][1].attack.cost`). Run 100 matches, not 50: `MAP=<n> DIFF=Normal tools/balance/runbatch.sh dp_m<n> 101 ... 120` for each map, then `tools/balance/winrates.py dp_m0 ... dp_m4`.
- **C. Not applied:** look at Stonekeep's castle sides (spawn walk, door lane, ice) before any number changes; a faction speed or regen change would also move the other four maps.
- **D. Not applied:** the Human Crossbow cooldown 0.62 -> 0.58 tried in `tuneA` did nothing measurable (Human ranged K/D 1.02 -> 0.95), so it was dropped.
- **E. Method:** judge faction or map balance on 100+ matches per setting, or make the demo deterministic first.
