## What was run
40 seeded all-bot matches (4v4, `--demo`), headless, `tools/balance/runbatch.sh` with the new `DIFF=Easy|Normal|Hard` option, tallied with `tools/balance/agg.py`. Seeds 11-14 at Normal and 11-12 at Easy and Hard, on every map: Wildwood (0), Moonlit Wildwood (1), Ember Pass (2), Stonekeep Fortress (3), Twilight Grove (4). The flag sets the difficulty of **every** bot in the match, so a run compares two equal teams, never a stronger one against a weaker one.

## Findings
1. **Factions are even.** 20-20 in wins over 40 matches, kills within 2%. No change recommended.
2. **Match length is fine.** Regulation is 600 s; the median match ends around 6.5 minutes and only 1 in 10 needs overtime. Ember Pass is the short one (about 220 s at Normal and Hard): worth a look if a "10-minute match" is promised on the store page.
3. **Melee trails ranged.** Pooled K/D: ranged Elves 1.02 and Humans 1.09; melee 0.91 and 0.67; support 0.84 and 1.35. The weakest classes with n of 15 or more: Humans Warden 0.72 (n=19), Vanguard 0.82 (15), Frostweaver 0.88 (23); Elves Pyromancer 0.87 (27), Cleric 0.70 (13). The strongest: Humans DarkPriest 1.46 and Cleric 1.36, Sharpshooter 1.43. Per-class samples are 13 to 27, so a gap of 0.2 to 0.3 is inside the noise; the melee-versus-ranged gap is the only pattern that repeats across both teams. `docs/balance/patch-8.md` already named Knights as the weakest trade and blamed the bot melee AI as much as the stats.
4. **Difficulty levels cannot be judged from bot-versus-bot runs.** Both teams scale together, so win rates stay near 50% by construction. What does show: Easy matches have fewer kills (31 per match against 40 Normal and 41 Hard) and more bot stalls per match (0.7 against 0.2 and 0.4). Hard ended 15 s sooner than Normal on average. Whether Easy is gentle enough and Hard hard enough needs people playing against the bots; see below.
5. **Economy.** Wood gathered per team per match: Wildwood 36, Moonlit 37, Twilight Grove 33, Stonekeep 22. Stonekeep gathers a third less (its matches were also the most spread out: 123 s to 610 s), with no placement warnings, so it is the map layout or shorter matches, not a bug found here. Ore is 0 everywhere because it is off on purpose (`ECONOMY.ore_on=false`, Faisal 2026-10-10). Ember Pass prints no economy line (not looked into why). Wildwood and Moonlit still place 8 of 9 wood pairs (one `no room` warning per match; `docs/steam-checklist.md`). Coin (gold) is an account reward, not a match resource, so there is nothing to balance in a bot match.
6. **Ranking up works as intended.** Promoted players beat fresh ones 39 to 6; the higher-level fighter wins 703 of 1,214 non-equal fights (58%).

## Proposed (not applied; Faisal decides)
- **A. Melee uplift, small.** If Faisal wants melee closer to ranged, start with Warden (damage 33 to 43 per match against 65 to 84 for the ranged classes). Run a six-seed batch per map on the change before merging: `MAP=<n> DIFF=Normal tools/balance/runbatch.sh <tag> 21 22 23 24 25 26`, then `tools/balance/agg.py <tag>`.
- **B. Difficulty needs a playtest, not a batch.** Faisal or a tester plays one match on each of Easy, Normal and Hard. If an asymmetric bot setting is wanted (one team Hard, one Easy), `bot_difficulty` would have to become per team; that is a code change, not done here.
- **C. Ember Pass length.** Decide whether a 220 s average is wanted; no change proposed.
- **D. Stonekeep wood.** Look at where its wood nodes sit before changing any cost.
