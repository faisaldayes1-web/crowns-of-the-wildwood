# Crowns of the Wildwood — store page candidate

## Short description

Steal the enemy monarch and carry them home in a cartoon fantasy battle. Rally as Elves or Humans, switch classes and protect your carrier in couch split-screen matches with bots.

## About the game

The enemy monarch is within reach. Getting them out of the castle is only half the job.

Crowns of the Wildwood brings Elves and Humans into lively battles over a very valuable passenger. Break through the defense, carry the opposing monarch to your throne and help your carrier survive the journey home. First to two captures wins; when the match clock runs out, the higher score takes the match. A tie moves into overtime.

Choose a class that suits the next push. Fight on the front line, support allies, challenge ranged threats or help the team hold a route. Changing the battle can mean changing your role rather than starting over.

Play local split-screen with friends or join the battle with bots. Bright castles, bold silhouettes and a cartoon tabletop world keep the objective at the center of the action.

- Elves-versus-Humans battles built around stealing the enemy monarch.
- Class choices for fighting, support and team play.
- Couch split-screen and bots.
- A colorful fantasy battlefield with castles, paths and clear team identity.
