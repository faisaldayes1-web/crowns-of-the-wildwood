# Crowns of the Wildwood — press-kit text candidate

## One-line pitch

A cartoon fantasy capture battle where stealing the enemy monarch is easier than getting them safely home.

## Short overview

Crowns of the Wildwood pits Elves and Humans against one another in a race to carry the opposing monarch back to the throne. Players choose combat and support roles, help a carrier across contested routes and defend their own castle. Local split-screen and bots put the action on the same couch; the cartoon tabletop setting gives each push a bright, readable backdrop.

## Longer overview

An opening in the castle defense can turn a quiet lane into the center of the match. One player carries the objective, another clears a path and a third holds off the pursuit. The team that adapts its roles and protects its route gets the monarch home.

Matches end at two captures or the clock, with classes supporting different ways to fight and help allies. The game pairs playful fantasy presentation with a concrete team objective: bring the monarch back, and keep your own out of enemy hands.
