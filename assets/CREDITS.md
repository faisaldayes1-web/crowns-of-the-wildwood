# Third-party assets

All of these are CC0 (public domain). Credit is appreciated by the authors but not required.

- **KayKit Adventurers Character Pack 1.0** (Knight, Mage, Rogue, Barbarian models, animations and gear) by Kay Lousberg, https://kaylousberg.com — CC0. `assets/characters/`, `assets/props/gear/`. Team colour skins in `assets/characters/skins/` are recoloured from the pack's palette textures by `tools/recolor_skins.py`.
- **KayKit Medieval Hexagon Pack 1.0** (buildings in the two team colours, windmills, mills, wells, markets, trees, hills, mountains, rocks, crates, tents, fences, flags, water lilies) by Kay Lousberg — CC0. `assets/props/hex/`.
- **KayKit Dungeon Remastered 1.0 / 1.1** (banners, torches, chests, columns, tables, chairs, shelves, kegs, barrels, bottles, candles, coins, beds) by Kay Lousberg — CC0. `assets/props/dungeon/`.
- **KayKit Halloween Bits 1.0** (lantern posts, crypt, graves, dead trees, autumn pines, pumpkins, benches, candles) by Kay Lousberg — CC0. `assets/props/halloween/`.
- **KayKit Furniture Bits 1.0** (tables, chairs, beds, shelves) by Kay Lousberg — CC0. `assets/props/furniture/`.
- **KayKit Restaurant Bits 1.0** (jars, bowls, pots, plates, stew, crates of food) by Kay Lousberg — CC0. `assets/props/kitchen/`.
- The stylised stone, cobble, wood, bark, grass and dirt textures in `assets/textures/` and the HUD icons in `assets/ui/icons/` are generated by this project's own scripts (`tools/make_textures.py`, `tools/make_icons.py`), so they carry no third-party licence. The hex pack palette in `assets/props/hex/` is recoloured by `tools/recolor_palette.py` (original kept as `hexagons_medieval_src.png`).
- `assets/ui/logo.png` is the game's own logo, and `assets/ui/cards/` (faction logos, crests and class portraits) is the game's own card art, both supplied by the project owner.
- **Luckiest Guy** font by Astigmatic (Brian J. Bonislawsky), from Google Fonts — Apache License 2.0 (`assets/fonts/LICENSE-LuckiestGuy.txt`). `assets/fonts/LuckiestGuy-Regular.ttf`, used for the big cartoon banner titles.
- `assets/ui/skills/` (hexagon skill tiles and the scoreboard shield faces) are cut from UI reference art supplied by the project owner.
- **Lilita One** font by Juan Montoreano, from Google Fonts — SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`assets/fonts/OFL-LilitaOne.txt`). `assets/fonts/LilitaOne-Regular.ttf`, used for the top bar text.
- `assets/ui/topbar/topbar.png` (the match top bar: banners, crest shields, clock frame, parchment strip) is cut from the owner's UI reference art by `tools/make_topbar.py`, with its text painted out.
- **Lilita One** (the menu lettering) by Juan Montoreano — SIL Open Font License 1.1, `assets/ui/fonts/` (licence text beside it). The menu frames, buttons, icons and map card art in `assets/ui/menu/` are drawn by `tools/make_menu_art.py` from this game's own renders.
- **Music** in `assets/music/` (menu theme, the three battle stems and the stingers) is original, composed note by note in `tools/make_music.py` for this game. It is rendered with the **FluidR3_GM** General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen — MIT licence (Debian/Ubuntu package `fluid-soundfont-gm`). The soundfont is only used at render time and is not shipped; only the rendered `.ogg` files are.
